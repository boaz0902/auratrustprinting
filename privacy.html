What we collect

  • Your name, contact number, preferred contact method, and pickup/delivery details
  • The files you upload for a quote and the service options you request
  • Your notes, order reference, estimated and final prices, and order status

Why we collect it

Only to review your files, prepare and complete your print request, contact you about your quote and order, and keep the order record for business purposes.

Who can see your data

Only authorized AuraTrust staff. We do not sell or share your information or files with anyone, except where required by law. Our website uses Supabase to store data and uploaded files.

How long we keep it

  • Uploaded files are automatically deleted 30 days after an order is marked Completed or Cancelled. AuraTrust can configure this retention period.
  • Order details are retained for business records. Ask us about access, correction, or deletion of your information.

Your rights
